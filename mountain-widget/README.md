# mountain-widget

A 3D topographic wireframe mountain animation. The page is self-contained: open [`topography-sculptor.html`](topography-sculptor.html) in a browser. There is no build step.

Moved from [apoideas/mountain-widget](https://github.com/apoideas/mountain-widget) at [`1f09975`](https://github.com/apoideas/mountain-widget/commit/1f09975e1999d46a823b8453327faf2dc957095f).

## Controls

- Drag empty space, or right-drag, to orbit. Scroll to zoom. Shift-scroll changes the pitch.
- On a touch screen, pinch with two fingers to zoom and pan.
- Click a hill to select it. Click empty space to clear the selection. Shift-click adds or removes a hill from the group, and dragging one selected hill moves the group.
- Drag the height or width handle on the active hill. A dragged hill wobbles when you let go.
- Double-click or double-tap empty space and hold to grow a new hill.
- Delete or Backspace removes the selected hills. The last hill stays.
